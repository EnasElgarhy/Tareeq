# Entrepreneurial Mindset Compass — Source Material
# بوصلة العقلية الريادية — المادة المصدرية

This is the raw author material for a custom assessment that maps a student's
entrepreneurial mindset. It defines the scoring dimensions, the question bank
(answer → dimension → points), and the result rubric (profiles + selection rules).

---

## Scoring Categories (dimensions)

| Code       | Name (EN)    | Name (AR)        |
|------------|--------------|------------------|
| VISION     | Vision       | الرؤية           |
| EXECUTION  | Execution    | التنفيذ          |
| RISK       | Risk Appetite| الإقدام على المخاطرة |
| INNOVATION | Innovation   | الابتكار         |
| PEOPLE     | People       | الناس            |

Every answer awards points to exactly one dimension (or none, for neutral
options). Points are 1–3 by how strongly the answer signals that dimension.

## Question Bank

### Q1 — single choice
**EN:** A new opportunity appears but the details are fuzzy. You:
**AR:** تظهر فرصة جديدة لكن تفاصيلها غير واضحة. أنت:

- A) Picture where it could go in five years → **VISION +3** / تتخيّل إلى أين قد تصل خلال خمس سنوات
- B) Map the first three concrete steps → **EXECUTION +3** / ترسم أول ثلاث خطوات عملية
- C) Just dive in and figure it out → **RISK +2** / تبدأ فورًا وتكتشف الطريق
- D) Call people who've done it before → **PEOPLE +2** / تتصل بمن جرّبوها من قبل

### Q2 — single choice
**EN:** Your team is stuck on a hard problem. Your instinct is to:
**AR:** فريقك عالق في مشكلة صعبة. غريزتك أن:

- A) Invent an approach nobody tried yet → **INNOVATION +3** / تبتكر أسلوبًا لم يجرّبه أحد
- B) Break it into a checklist and grind → **EXECUTION +2** / تقسّمها إلى قائمة وتنجزها
- C) Rally everyone and split the work → **PEOPLE +2** / تحفّز الجميع وتوزّع العمل
- D) Bet on a bold shortcut → **RISK +2** / تراهن على اختصار جريء

### Q3 — binary
**EN:** Would you rather launch something imperfect now, or perfect it first?
**AR:** هل تفضّل إطلاق شيء غير مكتمل الآن، أم إتقانه أولًا؟

- A) Launch now, learn fast → **RISK +3** / أطلق الآن وتعلّم بسرعة
- B) Perfect it first → **EXECUTION +2** / أتقنه أولًا

### Q4 — single choice
**EN:** What energizes you most about building something?
**AR:** ما الذي يحمّسك أكثر في بناء شيء ما؟

- A) The big idea and the "what if" → **VISION +3** / الفكرة الكبيرة و"ماذا لو"
- B) Making it actually work → **EXECUTION +3** / جعله يعمل فعلًا
- C) Doing it differently than everyone → **INNOVATION +3** / فعله بشكل مختلف عن الجميع
- D) Getting people excited to join → **PEOPLE +3** / إثارة حماس الناس للانضمام

### Q5 — single choice
**EN:** A startup you admire just raised millions. Your first thought:
**AR:** شركة ناشئة تعجبك جمعت ملايين للتو. أول ما يخطر ببالك:

- A) "What unmet need did they spot?" → **VISION +3** / "ما الحاجة غير الملبّاة التي اكتشفوها؟"
- B) "How did they ship so fast?" → **EXECUTION +2** / "كيف نفّذوا بهذه السرعة؟"
- C) "What did they do that's never been done?" → **INNOVATION +2** / "ما الذي فعلوه ولم يُفعل من قبل؟"
- D) "Who's on that founding team?" → **PEOPLE +2** / "من في فريق التأسيس؟"

### Q6 — scale (treat as single, 4 levels)
**EN:** "Uncertainty excites me more than it scares me."
**AR:** "عدم اليقين يحمّسني أكثر مما يخيفني."

- A) Strongly agree → **RISK +3** / أوافق بشدة
- B) Agree → **RISK +2** / أوافق
- C) Neutral → (no points) / محايد
- D) Disagree → **EXECUTION +1** / لا أوافق

### Q7 — single choice
**EN:** Pick the compliment that would mean the most:
**AR:** اختر المديح الذي سيعني لك أكثر:

- A) "You saw it before anyone else." → **VISION +3** / "رأيتها قبل الجميع."
- B) "You actually shipped it." → **EXECUTION +3** / "نفّذتها فعلًا."
- C) "Nobody thinks like you." → **INNOVATION +3** / "لا أحد يفكّر مثلك."
- D) "Everyone wanted to follow you." → **PEOPLE +3** / "أراد الجميع أن يتبعك."

### Q8 — binary
**EN:** Steady paycheck or your own venture?
**AR:** راتب ثابت أم مشروعك الخاص؟

- A) My own venture → **RISK +3** / مشروعي الخاص
- B) Steady paycheck → **EXECUTION +1** / راتب ثابت

### Q9 — single choice
**EN:** Given a blank whiteboard, you start by writing:
**AR:** أمام لوح أبيض فارغ، تبدأ بكتابة:

- A) The mission and the dream → **VISION +2** / الرسالة والحلم
- B) The plan and the deadlines → **EXECUTION +3** / الخطة والمواعيد النهائية
- C) Ten wild ideas → **INNOVATION +3** / عشر أفكار جريئة
- D) The list of people to call → **PEOPLE +2** / قائمة بمن ستتصل بهم

### Q10 — single choice
**EN:** People most often come to you for:
**AR:** غالبًا ما يأتي إليك الناس من أجل:

- A) A bold vision to get behind → **VISION +3** / رؤية جريئة يلتفّون حولها
- B) Getting things over the finish line → **EXECUTION +3** / إيصال الأمور إلى خط النهاية
- C) A fresh, unexpected angle → **INNOVATION +2** / زاوية جديدة غير متوقعة
- D) An introduction or a pep talk → **PEOPLE +3** / تعريف بشخص أو كلمة تحفيزية

### Q11 — single choice
**EN:** Your biggest risk so far has been:
**AR:** أكبر مخاطرة خضتها حتى الآن كانت:

- A) Backing an idea others doubted → **RISK +3** / دعم فكرة شكّ بها الآخرون
- B) Taking on more than I could handle → **RISK +2** / تحمّل أكثر مما أستطيع
- C) Trying a method no one taught me → **INNOVATION +2** / تجربة طريقة لم يعلّمني إياها أحد
- D) Speaking up to lead a room → **PEOPLE +2** / المبادرة لقيادة مجموعة

### Q12 — open text (not scored)
**EN:** Describe a problem in the world you'd genuinely love to solve.
**AR:** صِف مشكلة في العالم تودّ حقًّا حلّها.

## Result Rubric

**Strategy:** evaluate the rules below in order — **first match wins**. If no rule
matches, fall back to **The Explorer**.

### Profiles

**VISIONARY — The Visionary** (صاحب الرؤية) · dimension: VISION
Sees opportunity early and paints a future others want to build.
- Recommended majors: Business, Economics, Strategic Design
- Recommended careers: Founder/CEO, Product Visionary, Venture Builder
- Strengths: foresight, ambition, storytelling the future
- Development areas: day-to-day follow-through

**OPERATOR — The Operator** (المنفّذ) · dimension: EXECUTION
Turns plans into shipped reality with relentless follow-through.
- Recommended majors: Operations Management, Industrial Engineering, Business
- Recommended careers: COO, Operations Lead, Program Manager
- Strengths: discipline, reliability, getting it done
- Development areas: stepping back for the big bet

**TRAILBLAZER — The Trailblazer** (الرائد) · dimension: INNOVATION
Solves problems in ways nobody expected.
- Recommended majors: Computer Science, Industrial Design, R&D tracks
- Recommended careers: Inventor, R&D Lead, Innovation Strategist
- Strengths: originality, curiosity, lateral thinking
- Development areas: operational discipline

**RAINMAKER — The Rainmaker** (صانع الفرص) · dimension: PEOPLE
Opens doors and rallies people behind a goal.
- Recommended majors: Marketing, Communications, Business Development
- Recommended careers: Sales Lead, BD Manager, Community Builder
- Strengths: persuasion, networks, energy
- Development areas: solo deep work

**RISKTAKER — The Risk-Taker** (المغامر) · dimension: RISK
Thrives in uncertainty and acts while others hesitate.
- Recommended majors: Entrepreneurship, Finance, Economics
- Recommended careers: Startup Founder, Trader, Turnaround Lead
- Strengths: courage, speed, resilience
- Development areas: measured patience

**EXPLORER — The Explorer** (المستكشف) *(fallback)*
Still discovering — strong across several dimensions, no single dominant signal yet.
- Recommended majors: Interdisciplinary / Liberal Arts
- Recommended careers: Rotational / generalist programs
- Strengths: versatility, openness
- Development areas: choosing a primary direction

### Rules (first match wins)

1. IF RISK >= 10 AND RISK > EXECUTION THEN **The Risk-Taker**
2. IF VISION >= 11 THEN **The Visionary**
3. IF INNOVATION >= 10 AND INNOVATION > VISION THEN **The Trailblazer**
4. IF PEOPLE >= 10 THEN **The Rainmaker**
5. IF EXECUTION >= 10 THEN **The Operator**
6. (no match) → **The Explorer**

> Note for the importer: the scoring dimensions are VISION, EXECUTION, RISK,
> INNOVATION, PEOPLE. Each option maps to one dimension with the points shown.
> The rubric defines result profiles and the first-match rules that turn
> dimension totals into a single winning profile.
