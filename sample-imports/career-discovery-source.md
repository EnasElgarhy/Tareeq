# Career Discovery Assessment — Source Material

This document is the raw author material for a custom career-orientation assessment.
It contains the scoring categories, the question bank (with answer→category→points
mappings), and the result rubric (profiles + how scores select a profile).

---

## Scoring Categories (dimensions)

| Code     | Name (EN)        | Name (AR)        |
|----------|------------------|------------------|
| LEAD     | Leadership       | القيادة          |
| TECH     | Technology       | التقنية          |
| ENTR     | Entrepreneurship | ريادة الأعمال    |
| DESIGN   | Design & Creativity | التصميم والإبداع |
| ANALYTIC | Analytical       | التحليل          |
| COMM     | Communication    | التواصل          |

Every answer option awards points to exactly one category (or none, for neutral
options). Points are typically 1–3 based on how strongly the answer signals that
dimension.

## Question Bank

### Q1 — single choice
**EN:** When a group project starts, what role do you naturally take?
**AR:** عندما يبدأ مشروع جماعي، ما الدور الذي تتولاه بطبيعتك؟

- A) I organize the team and set the direction → **LEAD +3**
- B) I figure out the tools and technical setup → **TECH +2**
- C) I sketch how it should look and feel → **DESIGN +2**
- D) I make sure everyone understands each other → **COMM +2**

### Q2 — single choice
**EN:** You have a free weekend to learn something new. You pick:

- A) Building a small app or automation → TECH +3
- B) A public-speaking or storytelling workshop → COMM +3
- C) How to launch a tiny side business → ENTR +3
- D) A digital illustration or UI design course → DESIGN +3

### Q3 — single choice
**EN:** A dataset lands on your desk with no instructions. You:

- A) Start charting it to find patterns → ANALYTIC +3
- B) Ask who the stakeholders are and what decision it informs → LEAD +2
- C) Build a script to clean and process it → TECH +2
- D) Wonder how to present it so people care → COMM +1

### Q4 — binary
**EN:** Would you rather pitch an idea to investors than debug code for a day?

- A) Yes, pitch the idea → ENTR +2
- B) No, I'd take the debugging → TECH +2

### Q5 — single choice
**EN:** What kind of praise means the most to you?

- A) "You really held this team together." → LEAD +3
- B) "That was elegant — how did you build it?" → TECH +2
- C) "I couldn't stop looking at it." → DESIGN +3
- D) "You explained that so clearly." → COMM +2

### Q6 — single choice
**EN:** You read about a successful startup. Your first thought is:

- A) "What's the business model?" → ENTR +3
- B) "What's the tech stack?" → TECH +2
- C) "Who led that team?" → LEAD +2
- D) "How did they design the experience?" → DESIGN +2

### Q7 — scale (treat as single choice, 4 levels)
**EN:** "I enjoy breaking a messy problem into clean, logical steps."

- A) Strongly agree → ANALYTIC +3
- B) Agree → ANALYTIC +2
- C) Neutral → (no points)
- D) Disagree → COMM +1

### Q8 — single choice
**EN:** Pick the class you'd never skip:

- A) Entrepreneurship & innovation → ENTR +3
- B) Computer science → TECH +3
- C) Statistics & data → ANALYTIC +3
- D) Visual design → DESIGN +3

### Q9 — single choice
**AR:** أي بيئة عمل تناسبك أكثر؟ (Which work environment suits you best?)

- A) قيادة فريق نحو هدف (Leading a team toward a goal) → LEAD +3
- B) العمل بعمق على مشكلة تقنية (Deep work on a technical problem) → TECH +3
- C) إطلاق شيء جديد خاص بي (Launching something of my own) → ENTR +2
- D) صياغة رسالة تلهم الناس (Crafting a message that inspires) → COMM +2

### Q10 — single choice
**EN:** Your ideal contribution to a product is:

- A) The vision and roadmap → LEAD +2
- B) The architecture and code → TECH +3
- C) The look, feel, and flow → DESIGN +3
- D) The metrics and experiments → ANALYTIC +2

### Q11 — single choice
**EN:** When you hit a wall, you tend to:

- A) Rally people and delegate → LEAD +2
- B) Research and prototype a fix → TECH +2
- C) Reframe the problem creatively → DESIGN +2

### Q12 — single choice
**EN:** Which headline would you be proudest to earn?

- A) "Founder turns idea into thriving company" → ENTR +3
- B) "Engineer ships breakthrough feature" → TECH +2
- C) "Designer reimagines everyday experience" → DESIGN +3
- D) "Leader transforms struggling team" → LEAD +3

### Q13 — single choice
**EN:** You're happiest communicating through:

- A) A persuasive talk or story → COMM +3
- B) A clean diagram or dashboard → ANALYTIC +2
- C) A polished visual or mockup → DESIGN +2
- D) A clear plan others can follow → LEAD +2

### Q14 — binary
**EN:** Risk or stability — which pulls you more?

- A) Risk and ownership → ENTR +3
- B) Stability and craft → TECH +1

### Q15 — single choice
**EN:** People most often come to you for:

- A) A decision or direction → LEAD +3

## Result Rubric

**Strategy:** evaluate the threshold rules below in order (first match wins). If
no rule matches, fall back to **The Explorer**.

### Profiles

**LEADER — The Strategist** (الاستراتيجي)
Natural organizer who sets direction and aligns people.
- Recommended majors: Business Administration, Public Policy, Organizational Psychology
- Recommended careers: Product Manager, Operations Lead, Management Consultant
- Strengths: vision, decisiveness, motivating others
- Development areas: delegating detail, patience with process

**BUILDER — The Builder** (الباني)
Hands-on technologist who loves making things work.
- Recommended majors: Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering
- Recommended careers: Software Engineer, ML Engineer, Systems Architect
- Strengths: logical depth, persistence, systems thinking
- Development areas: communicating to non-technical people

**FOUNDER — The Founder** (المؤسس)
Opportunity-seeker energized by ownership and risk.
- Recommended majors: Entrepreneurship, Business, Economics
- Recommended careers: Startup Founder, Product Owner, Venture Analyst
- Strengths: initiative, resilience, big-picture bets
- Development areas: operational follow-through

**CREATOR — The Creator** (المبدع)
Design-minded maker focused on experience and aesthetics.
- Recommended majors: Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Digital Media
- Recommended careers: Product Designer, UX Designer, Creative Director
- Strengths: empathy, taste, visual problem-solving
- Development areas: quantitative rigor

**ANALYST — The Analyst** (المحلل)
Pattern-finder who turns data into decisions.
- Recommended majors: Statistics, Data Science, Applied Mathematics
- Recommended careers: Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Quantitative Researcher
- Strengths: rigor, objectivity, structured thinking
- Development areas: storytelling with data

**CONNECTOR — The Connector** (الموصِّل)
Communicator who aligns people through clarity and persuasion.
- Recommended majors: Communications, Marketing, Media Studies
- Recommended careers: Marketing Lead, Communications Manager, Brand Strategist
- Strengths: empathy, persuasion, clarity
- Development areas: technical depth

**EXPLORER — The Explorer** (المستكشف) *(fallback)*
Still discovering — strong across several areas, no single dominant signal yet.
- Recommended majors: Liberal Arts, Interdisciplinary Studies
- Recommended careers: Rotational / generalist programs
- Strengths: versatility, curiosity
- Development areas: focusing on a primary direction

### Rules (first match wins)

1. IF ENTR >= 12 THEN **The Founder**
2. IF TECH >= 12 AND TECH > LEAD THEN **The Builder**
3. IF LEAD >= 12 THEN **The Strategist**
4. IF DESIGN >= 10 AND DESIGN > TECH THEN **The Creator**
5. IF ANALYTIC >= 10 THEN **The Analyst**
6. IF COMM >= 9 THEN **The Connector**
7. (no match) → **The Explorer**

> Note for the importer: categories LEAD, TECH, ENTR, DESIGN, ANALYTIC, COMM are
> the scoring dimensions. Each question's options map to one of them with the
> points shown. The rubric above defines the result profiles and the rules that
> turn category totals into a single winning profile.
