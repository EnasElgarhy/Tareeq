How it feels.

light-mode questions

A quieter reading room.

V1 used the dark plum surface for everything around the assessment — start, intro, the Contract of Honesty — but switched to a cream surface specifically inside the question screens. The result is an editorial reading experience: long-form question text sits on a soft cream card, answer pills carry their own colored icon, and Kai stays as a small character anchor rather than the dominant presence.

What's different from the v2 night flow

  • Surface · cream paper, ink type.
  • Question card · serif italic on a tall pill, not a speech bubble with a tail.
  • Answer pills · each has a colored icon tile (coral plus, cyan drop, lavender leaf, blush heart, plum lightning) on white.
  • Audio row · coral play button, neutral mute and speed pills.
  • Compass · top-center, small coral wedge fills with progress.

The argument for it

The questions are the slow, considered part of the journey. A cream surface signals "this is reading time" — different register from the ceremony that surrounds it.

What stays consistent across all six

The compass in the header, the pillar label eyebrow, the cream question card with serif italic, the coral play button anchoring the audio row, and the white answer pills with their tone-coded icon tiles on the left. The system holds together visually whether the question is two options or five.
