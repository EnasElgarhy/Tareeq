Fun Facts for Assessment Break Screens
Interesting Career Data to Include After Every 10 Questions

Overview
After questions 10, 20, 30, the assessment shows a “Fun Fact” screen to: - Break up the monotony of 40 questions - Keep users engaged - Provide valuable, surprising information - Make the wait feel purposeful
Design principle: Each fact should be surprising, inspiring, or counterintuitive. Avoid obvious statements like “Engineering pays well.”

Implementation Guidelines
Format for Display:
Each fun fact screen should show: - 🎯 Icon (emoji or graphic element) - “Did you know?” header - Bold statement (the surprising fact) - Brief context (why it matters) - Source (builds credibility)

Rotation Strategy:
After Q10: Show one of Fact Set 1 (randomly selected)
After Q20: Show one of Fact Set 2 (randomly selected)
After Q30: Show one of Fact Set 3 (randomly selected)
Why random rotation: - Users who retake assessment see different facts - Keeps content fresh for multiple uses - Some facts are region-specific (e.g., GCC vs North Africa)

Tone Guidelines:
✅ DO: - Use surprising, counterintuitive facts (“Art History beats Computer Science!”) - Make it personal (“Your generation is…”) - Be specific with numbers (“55% of GCC youth,” not “many young people”) - Keep it short (2-3 sentences max) - End with implication (“…making it one of the fastest-growing workforces”)
❌ DON’T: - Use obvious statements (“Engineering is a good career”) - Be preachy (“You should study hard”) - Overwhelm with data (“In 2019 the rate was X, in 2020 it was Y…”) - Use jargon (“labor force participation rate”)

Arabic Translation Notes:
Each fact needs Arabic translation. Key considerations:
Numbers: Keep as Western numerals (55%, not ٥٥٪) even in Arabic text
Sources: Keep source names in English (e.g., “World Bank”) with Arabic translation of the fact
Tone: Arabic version should maintain the same casual, surprising tone
Example:
English: “Did you know? 55% of GCC youth plan to start their own business in the next 5 years — the highest entrepreneurship rate in the world.”
Arabic: “هل تعلم؟ 55% من شباب دول مجلس التعاون الخليجي يخططون لبدء أعمالهم الخاصة خلال السنوات الخمس القادمة — أعلى معدل ريادة أعمال في العالم.”

Mobile UI Considerations:
Screen size: Fun fact screen should: - Use large, bold font for the main fact - Icon at top (adds visual interest) - “Did you know?” in accent color (Origin Coral) - Source in small, muted text at bottom - Sufficient white space (don’t cram)

Testing Recommendations:
Before launch, test with 5-7 users aged 16-22:
Show them 3 fun facts
Ask:
“Did this surprise you?”
“Did this make you more or less motivated?”
“Would you remember this fact?”
“Would you share this with a friend?”
Goal: 80%+ should find at least one fact surprising or interesting.
