Two-Tier Report Design System
Student Version (Visual & Scannable) + Parent Version (Detailed & Comprehensive)

Why This Approach is Critical
The Problem with One-Size-Fits-All:
❌ For Students (16-22): - 500-600 words of text = instant scroll-past on mobile - Gen Z processes information visually (TikTok, Instagram, infographics) - Attention span optimized for rapid scanning, not essay reading - Want quick answers: “What should I study? What jobs exist? What next?”
❌ For Parents: - Visual summary alone feels too superficial - Want to understand the reasoning behind the recommendation - Need depth to have career conversations with their child - May want to share with school counselors or advisors
The Solution: Two-Tier System
✅ Student Version (Primary): - Visual, scannable, mobile-first - 90 seconds to fully understand - Shareable on social media - Looks like a premium product (think Spotify Wrapped, not a school report)
✅ Parent Version (Secondary): - Full narrative (our 500-600 word framework) - In-depth explanations - Research-backed reasoning - Can be downloaded/printed as PDF

Student Version Design: “The Visual Report”
Design Principle:
“If you can’t understand it in 90 seconds by scrolling on your phone, it’s too complex.”

Page 6: Understanding the CORE Model
ABOUT THE CORE ASSESSMENT

The CORE Assessment uses a four-pillar model to provide
comprehensive career guidance:

1. CURIOSITIES (What interests them)
   Identifies which fields genuinely engage your child's
   natural interests through 16 questions.

2. OPERATIONS (How they work)
   Determines their cognitive and execution style:
   - Processing: Structured vs Flexible
   - Focus: Deep vs Broad

3. REWARDS (What motivates them)
   Identifies core drivers:
   - Recognition, Impact, Autonomy, Mastery, or Stability

4. ECOSYSTEMS (Where they thrive)
   Determines preferred working conditions:
   - Social: Collaborative vs Independent
   - Pulse: Dynamic vs Predictable

This is more comprehensive than traditional interest inventories
(like Holland RIASEC) which only measure interests, not how
someone works, what motivates them, or where they thrive.

Summary: What Makes This Work
For Students:
✅ Visual, not textual ✅ Scannable in 60-90 seconds ✅ Actionable (clear next steps) ✅ Shareable (looks premium) ✅ Mobile-first (where they actually are)
For Parents:
✅ Comprehensive (full context) ✅ Research-backed (shows methodology) ✅ Printable (PDF format) ✅ Detailed (addresses their questions)
For You (Business):
✅ Both audiences satisfied ✅ Download PDF = email capture ✅ Share feature = organic marketing ✅ Premium feel = justifies future paid features

This two-tier approach gives teenagers the quick visual hit they need while giving parents the depth they want. Best of both worlds!
